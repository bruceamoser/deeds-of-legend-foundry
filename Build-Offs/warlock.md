## D&D play loop and limits

Use empowered Eldritch Blast as the reliable attack, reserve Pact slots for curse/defense, and use the Tome’s rituals to make the bargain useful beyond combat.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- The patron is established in fiction; its mechanical subclass begins at level 3.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 8; Dex: 14; Con: 13; Int: 10; Wis: 12; Cha: 15; background increases Cha: 2; Wis: 1. HP calculation: 8 + 5 + 2 × CON 1. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +4.

### Signature: Borrowed Tongue

**Action:** Maneuver in combat, or one minute out of combat

**Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self; one language you hear spoken.

A spirit that knows the language possesses you. You understand and speak that language for the scene. You do not read or write it.

**Duration:** Until the end of the scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** Once per scene; one language at a time.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Arcane Bolt, Command, Borrowed Tongue, the chosen familiar spell; keep the rest of the occult utility in its books.

**Combat:** Arcane Bolt is the at-will attack, but it rolls Knowledge +0 here. Command and other occult workings use their printed attributes, including Reason −1 where specified. The large Guile-based SP reserve does not improve those rolls. Borrowed Tongue uses a Maneuver in combat and grants spoken comprehension, not reading.

**Away from combat:** Borrowed spoken languages, illusions, hand manipulation and familiar magic make the pact useful away from combat. Follow the familiar spell’s actual limits; no independent extra combat turn or free advanced companion is assumed.

**Tradeoffs:** Odd charges 2 DP for every new discipline; Force costs two of the nine available DP. Recovery I restores only 1 SP once between safe overnight rests. Oath-Bound traits supply the bargain fiction but do not create unprinted spell invocations.

**Test this:** Run a second encounter without overnight recovery and ask the player to negotiate in an unfamiliar language. Does the occult utility compensate for weaker attack attributes and a different resource rhythm?

### Gaps kept visible

- Arcane Bolt is the reliable attack cantrip; it has no Eldritch Blast-specific scaling or invocation rider.
- Recovery I restores only 1 SP once between overnight rests, not a renewable Pact Magic short-rest pool.
- The pact is represented by Oath-Bound ancestry, spirit magic and Borrowed Tongue; Odd is a thematic fit rather than a dedicated warlock class.

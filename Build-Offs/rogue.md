## D&D play loop and limits

Scout and open locks, find an eligible Sneak Attack, then hide or disengage. The small silhouette and mobility keep this a skirmisher rather than a stand-up fighter.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- Duplicate background/class Thieves’ Tools proficiency is counted once; no invented replacement benefit.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 8; Dex: 15; Con: 14; Int: 13; Wis: 12; Cha: 10; background increases Dex: 2; Int: 1. HP calculation: 8 + 5 + 2 × CON 2. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +5.

### Signature: Blade in the Back

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One creature within reach that has not noticed you or is adjacent to one of your allies.

Make an Agility roll against the target. Weak: 3 physical damage. Standard: 4 physical damage, and the target has Bleeding 1. Strong: 6 physical damage, and the target has Bleeding 2.

**Duration:** Immediate; Bleeding uses its normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A melee weapon.

**Use limit:** Repeatable; normal action economy applies.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Blade in the Back, Quiet Passage, Patient Hand, Read the Snare, Running Vault; keep Light Fingers and universal Hide/Disengage available.

**Combat:** When unnoticed or when the target is adjacent to an ally, use Blade in the Back as the Main Action. Its 3/4/6 damage and Bleeding outcomes replace the attack result; do not add a Basic Melee attack on top. Use positioning and the universal action rules to create the next opportunity.

**Away from combat:** Quiet Passage III extends its stealth Boon to one adjacent companion; Patient Hand works on nonmagical locks with the purchased lock tools. Read the Snare identifies danger before disarming. A learned entry does not bypass impossible locks or absent evidence.

**Tradeoffs:** The build leans on its free signature for offense and spends nine DP on infiltration. Marksmanship is studied for future growth but grants no selected ranged technique, and the hero carries no bow. No light armor benefit is assumed.

**Test this:** Run a two-person infiltration and an encounter where no ally begins beside the enemy. Can the rogue create its opening without the DA granting an automatic surprise attack?

### Gaps kept visible

- Blade in the Back replaces, rather than adds to, a normal attack. It captures opportunistic striking without a separate Sneak Attack die.
- No light armor is worn and no ranged weapon is purchased; Marksmanship is a future study option.

## D&D play loop and limits

Solve obstacles with tools and temporary gear, equip the party with replicated items, and deliver a dependable wand-assisted cantrip while retaining a few support spells.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- 2025 final Artificer numeric tables and item plans were cross-checked in two independent secondary transcriptions because the full publisher class page is access-restricted. Publisher overview confirms the feature framework. No Unearthed Arcana draft is treated as final.
- Smith’s Tools proficiency is selected but that extra tool set is not owned; buy or borrow it before smithing.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 8; Dex: 14; Con: 13; Int: 15; Wis: 12; Cha: 10; background increases Int: 2; Con: 1. HP calculation: 8 + 5 + 2 × CON 2. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +2.

### Signature: Spot the Leverage

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature or obstacle within 5 squares; you and each ally within 5 squares who can hear and understand you.

Choose one visible creature or obstacle within 5 squares. Learn one useful opening: for a creature, one of its defenses or one visible weakness; for an obstacle, whether Force can overcome it and its remaining progress target. You and each ally within 5 squares who can hear and understand you gain 1 Boon to your next action against the target before the start of your next turn.

**Duration:** Information persists; each Boon expires when used or the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** One application per target per scene.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Arcane Bolt, Spot the Leverage, Magic Weapon, Mend and Fit; keep recipes and object utility in their stores.

**Combat:** Spot the Leverage is a Maneuver that supplies information and a short-lived Boon for actions against its target. Pair it with a learned spell attack when appropriate. Sustaining Magic Weapon instead uses that Maneuver, so the two benefits are not free simultaneous upkeep.

**Away from combat:** The hero has Craft Tools, a Material Bundle, four actual recipe choices and repair training. Mending and mundane repair solve different kinds of damage. Ordinary recipes still require their materials, work intervals and output caps. Selected recipes: Bound Torches, Twisted Cord, Travel Food and Wooden Brace.

**Tradeoffs:** Force costs 2 DP before any spellbook learning. Eight spells plus crafting create broad utility, but Magic Weapon is temporary and sustained. No permanent enchanted item, construct companion or arbitrary gadget is granted merely by the Craft/Force combination.

**Test this:** Ask the player to prepare a useful item for an ally and then leave it working while doing something else. That distinguishes a genuine persistent-item subsystem from a spell with workshop flavor.

### Gaps kept visible

- Magic Weapon is a sustained temporary enchantment; ordinary crafting plus Force does not create permanent replicated magic items.
- No construct companion is assumed at this level.

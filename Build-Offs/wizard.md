## D&D play loop and limits

Prepare a focused combat kit, protect yourself with Mage Armor/Shield, and solve exploration problems through the spellbook’s unprepared rituals.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- Eight book spells = six at level 1 plus two at level 2; only five class spells prepared. Mage Armor and familiar casting costs are not ignored.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 8; Dex: 14; Con: 13; Int: 15; Wis: 12; Cha: 10; background increases Int: 2; Con: 1. HP calculation: 6 + 4 + 2 × CON 2. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +2.

### Signature: Study the Pattern

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One ongoing magical effect within 5 squares that you are aware of.

Learn its origin and purpose, whether a creature or object sustains it, and its duration if it has one. You also become aware of how to end the effect if it is possible. Gain 1 Boon to your next roll using that information before the scene ends.

**Duration:** Information remains known; the Boon ends when used or the scene ends.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** One Boon from a given effect per scene.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Arcane Bolt, Magic Missile, Grease, Study the Pattern; keep rituals and object utility accessible in their books.

**Combat:** Use the chosen spell’s Main Action and SP cost, with Study the Pattern available as a Maneuver when its ongoing-effect target exists. If sustaining a spell, that Maneuver is already committed. Arcane Bolt supplies an unlimited fallback rather than consuming the reserve.

**Away from combat:** Mending, Unseen Servant, Mage Hand, Detect Magic and the lore technique give multiple concrete solutions. Study the Pattern gives information about an ongoing effect; it is not permission to learn every spell, identify every hidden object, or cast an unlearned solution.

**Tradeoffs:** Knowledge +2 improves core spell rolls, DP and Arcanist SP. Eleven DP buys twelve spells, a lore technique and capacity/recovery. Fixed pairs sometimes give unwanted spells, but there is no additional daily preparation limit; the D&D spellbook and copying economy are not assumed.

**Test this:** Present an unfamiliar magical obstacle after several paid casts. Test whether the player can select a useful known tool without scrolling through every spell or requesting an unlearned spell by analogy.

### Gaps kept visible

- HoL spellbooks unlock fixed pairs of spells; there is no free choice of two individual spells per level or copied-spell economy.
- Recovery I is a small once-per-rest SP recovery, not D&D Arcane Recovery.

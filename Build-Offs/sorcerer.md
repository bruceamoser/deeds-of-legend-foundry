## D&D play loop and limits

Unleash innate magic, bend casting with Metamagic, and alternate a reliable cantrip with a few decisive spells. Background healing reflects a temple-raised prodigy.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- Do not assume a bloodline subclass at level 2; 2024 sorcerers choose it at level 3.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 8; Dex: 14; Con: 13; Int: 10; Wis: 12; Cha: 15; background increases Cha: 2; Wis: 1. HP calculation: 6 + 4 + 2 × CON 1. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +2.

### Signature: Bent Trajectory

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One of your spell attacks; choose one visible, unoccupied square within 6 squares of you before rolling.

Choose one of your spell attacks. It can originate from any visible, unoccupied square within 6 squares of you. Determine cover and attack path from that square, but measure range from you. You must know the target’s location. Roll the spell attack normally.

**Duration:** For that spell.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** Repeatable; normal action economy applies.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Fire Bolt, Burning Hands, Feather Fall, Heroism; Bent Trajectory applies to eligible spell attacks rather than becoming another button.

**Combat:** Choose a legal alternate origin for a spell attack through Bent Trajectory, still measuring range from the caster and knowing the target’s location. Use the actual spell action and cost. This does not grant extra targets, extra actions or a universal Metamagic menu.

**Away from combat:** Boil Liquid, air manipulation and water creation solve environmental problems. Feather Fall is a situational response. Still Water’s concentration wording does not create a new sustain roll or waive the Maneuver.

**Tradeoffs:** The build spends three DP on Soul Capacity I–II and one on Recovery I. Knowledge is only +1 while Guile is +2, preserving charisma at a cost to Knowledge-based spells and the Arcanist contribution. Learned spells use their own printed attributes; Guile does not replace them globally.

**Test this:** Compare a direct fire attack with a creative alternate-origin attack. Does the signature provide enough expressive freedom to distinguish this hero from a Wizard with the same books?

### Gaps kept visible

- Bent Trajectory gives a distinctive free spell-shaping identity; it is not a flexible Metamagic selection.
- Arcanist SP scales with Knowledge rather than Guile: this charismatic innate caster pays a real resource cost for its stat emphasis.

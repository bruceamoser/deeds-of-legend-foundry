### Signature: Come Back

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One ally within 3 squares.

Trigger: one ally within 3 squares enters Dying. Move up to 3 squares to a square adjacent to that ally, without provoking. Then stabilize it. Stabilization clears both death roll tallies and restores no HP; the scar remains if the ally survives. This cannot restore the dead.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** Repeatable; uses your Reaction.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Entangle, Ray of Frost, Cure Wounds, Gentle Hand, Come Back; keep the broader nature utility in spellbooks.

**Combat:** Use Entangle to change the ground or Ray of Frost for offense. Sustaining Entangle occupies the Maneuver. Come Back is a rescue Reaction when an ally enters Dying, not extra healing or an animal transformation.

**Away from combat:** Goodberry, Speak with Plants, water creation, earth work and Gentle Hand make nature matter outside combat. Alter Self offers its printed adaptations or disguise; it does not provide a beast form or a new creature stat block.

**Tradeoffs:** Ten DP produces fourteen spells plus animal handling and a rescue signature. The capability is broad, but the decision load is substantial. A nature-specific signature may feel better than a general Shepherd rescue; that is a design choice, not permission to rewrite Come Back.

**Test this:** Present a narrow animal-sized scouting route and a negotiation with a frightened beast. The first reveals the Wild Shape gap; the second tests the available nature identity.

### Gaps kept visible

- Alter Self provides gills, swimming adaptation, or a changed face/voice; it does not provide an animal form. Wild Shape is a substantive missing trope at this level.
- Come Back provides a rescue Reaction rather than a nature-specific signature.

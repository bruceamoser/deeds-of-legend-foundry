## D&D play loop and limits

A former temple singer rallies allies, controls a troublesome enemy, heals at range, and handles negotiation. Inspiration and spells complement a broad skill suite.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- Acolyte is a deliberately SRD-only, temple-performer origin; the bard’s musical identity comes from class skills, instruments and magic.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 8; Dex: 14; Con: 13; Int: 10; Wis: 12; Cha: 15; background increases Cha: 2; Wis: 1. HP calculation: 8 + 5 + 2 × CON 1. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +4.

### Signature: Follow My Signal

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Up to two allies within 3 squares that can see or hear your Aid signal.

When you use Aid, you may assist two allies instead of one. Name the task for each and explain how your signal helps. Each gains Aid’s normal 1 Boon to its next matching roll before the start of your next turn. Outside combat, Aid still replaces your own contribution to a group roll.

**Duration:** Each Boon expires when used or the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** One two-recipient Aid on your turn.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Vicious Mockery, Heroism, universal Aid with Follow My Signal, Steady Voice, Make the Case; keep illusion and disguise spells in the book.

**Combat:** Use Vicious Mockery for offense or Aid to enable allies. Follow My Signal lets that Aid help two allies, with named tasks and an expiry. A sustained Heroism uses the Maneuver for upkeep; it competes with Steady Voice and other Maneuvers. The inspiration benefit is not a stored die that allies can spend much later.

**Away from combat:** Make the Case II and Plausible Story cover distinct honest and deceptive approaches. Disguise Self, illusions, Message and Comprehend Languages add practical social tools without making the DA’s social objective automatic.

**Tradeoffs:** Twelve DP supports broad skills and ten spells. That is close to the original overload concern if all are displayed as favorite buttons. The character has no armor training and no restoration spells; taking those would displace some social or illusion capability.

**Test this:** Ask the player to support two allies, then negotiate with a suspicious gatekeeper. Track whether the Aid expiry and sustain competition remain understandable.

### Gaps kept visible

- Follow My Signal improves universal Aid; it is not a stored Bardic Inspiration die.
- This build buys no armor training and has no restoration magic.

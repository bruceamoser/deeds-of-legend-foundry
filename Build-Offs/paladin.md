## D&D play loop and limits

Hold the line in armor, heal with Lay on Hands, and deliver a radiant finishing blow. The oath is story identity; a mechanical subclass arrives later.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 15; Dex: 12; Con: 13; Int: 8; Wis: 10; Cha: 14; background increases Str: 2; Con: 1. HP calculation: 10 + 6 + 2 × CON 2. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +3.

### Signature: Step Between

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing ally, after a single-target attack is declared against it but before rolling.

You and the ally swap squares. You become the target of the attack and resolve it against your defense. If either of you cannot occupy the other’s square, you cannot use this reaction. This does not redirect area attacks.

**Duration:** The triggering attack.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** Repeatable; uses your Reaction.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Measured Cut, Step Between, Cure Wounds, Magic Weapon, Blade Ward; retain universal Shield Block.

**Combat:** Use Magic Weapon for its printed temporary blessing, then sustain with a Maneuver while attacking. Step Between redirects an eligible single-target attack by swapping with an adjacent willing ally; Blade Ward and Shield Block compete for that Reaction. Healing or blessing with hand requirements may require freeing the weapon hand.

**Away from combat:** Healing and Light give the warrior something practical to do away from combat. The Oath-Bound identity is consequential fiction, but its broad invocation traits still need the audit’s trigger clarification.

**Tradeoffs:** Force costs this Protector 3 DP, plus 1 for Imbuement I. That is half the starting budget for the blessing route. Magic Weapon remains physical damage and is not Divine Smite. This build buys light armor; the heavy-armored ideal is deliberately delayed by the level-3 gate.

**Test this:** Offer a choice between preserving a sustained blessing and a useful Maneuver. Check whether the player wants that ongoing commitment or instead expects a one-hit expenditure of holy power.

### Gaps kept visible

- Magic Weapon is a sustained physical-damage blessing, not Divine Smite or radiant damage.
- Step Between, magical healing, and the weapon blessing capture the protector identity; heavy armor and burst smiting remain gaps.

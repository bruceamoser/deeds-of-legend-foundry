## D&D play loop and limits

Track the quarry with Survival Expertise, open with Hunter’s Mark, and use accurate bow fire while guiding the party through wilderness.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 12; Dex: 15; Con: 13; Int: 10; Wis: 14; Cha: 8; background increases Dex: 2; Con: 1. HP calculation: 10 + 6 + 2 × CON 2. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +3.

### Signature: Steady Aim

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self; one visible creature.

Aim at one visible creature. Until you move, you gain 1 Boon to all attacks you make against that creature and ignore partial cover from it.

**Duration:** Until you move.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A ranged weapon and ammunition.

**Use limit:** Repeatable; normal action economy applies.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Measured Shot, Watchful Shot, Steady Aim, Read the Trail, Wild Harvest; keep water and earth magic in the book.

**Combat:** Aim at a visible enemy with the Maneuver, then attack with Measured Shot II. Remaining still preserves Steady Aim; movement ends it. Watchful Shot supplies the ranged Reaction only at its printed movement trigger, using the ready bow and ammunition.

**Away from combat:** Track physical evidence, forage in suitable terrain, create water or work loose earth. Those tasks have different time intervals and environmental requirements; magical utility does not automatically complete a tracking objective.

**Tradeoffs:** No animal companion or Hunter’s Mark is assumed. Steady Aim improves attacks against the chosen target and ignores partial cover, but has a movement cost. The bow and ammunition are actual purchased inventory, and there is no unbought armor training.

**Test this:** Make the best firing position become unsafe. The decision to move and lose aim should feel like a useful tactical choice rather than punishment for behaving like a ranger.

### Gaps kept visible

- Steady Aim supports a quarry-focused archer but ends when the hero moves; it is not Hunter’s Mark.
- No animal companion is assumed.

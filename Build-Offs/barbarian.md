## D&D play loop and limits

Rage, commit to a powerful melee hit, use Reckless Attack when the extra exposure is worthwhile, and absorb a dangerous hit with Stone’s Endurance.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

### Signature: Go Berserk

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

Enter a rage until the end of the scene. While raging, you gain 1 Boon to attack rolls, have 1 Bane to defense rolls, and gain DR 1. You can end the rage at will. It also ends if you fall unconscious or enter Dying.

**Duration:** Until the end of the scene, or until you end it.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** Once per scene.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Driving Blow, Go Berserk, Running Vault, Catch Your Balance; keep basic attacks and Opportunity Attack available.

**Combat:** Enter Go Berserk with the Maneuver, then use Driving Blow as the Main Action. At III, Strong pushes 2 and also applies Dazed 1. On later turns the Maneuver can support a Running Vault after its movement requirement. Catch Your Balance competes with other Reactions rather than granting an additional response.

**Away from combat:** Long Reach helps with objects and non-attack touch tasks, not longer melee attack reach. Built for the Weather supports travel. This build can contribute physical solutions without purchasing a separate menu of attacks.

**Tradeoffs:** Seven DP buys one deep attack and two complementary choices. The free Armor discipline remains future potential: this hero has not learned armor training. Go Berserk is once per scene, not D&D’s replenishing limited Rage pool. Its printed untyped DR is a review question; do not stack it with armor by assumption.

**Test this:** Can the player explain the rage’s defense Bane and remember both Strong riders without a lookup? Compare a second encounter before rest, not just the first turn.

### Gaps kept visible

- Go Berserk prints untyped DR 1; its damage coverage needs the signature audit ruling.
- Rage is a scene-long Maneuver usable once per scene, with a defense Bane, rather than a limited daily resource.

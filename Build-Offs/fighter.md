## D&D play loop and limits

Hold the front line with flail, shield and Sap mastery; use Action Surge for a decisive second action and Second Wind to remain standing. Greatsword is an alternative loadout, not a simultaneous shield attack.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 15; Dex: 13; Con: 14; Int: 8; Wis: 12; Cha: 10; background increases Str: 2; Dex: 1. HP calculation: 10 + 6 + 2 × CON 2. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +4.

### Signature: Measured Guard

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, before a defense against an adjacent enemy.

Gain 1 Boon to the defense. After the attack resolves, if the defense was Strong and the attacker is within your reach, make a Basic Melee attack against that attacker. This counterattack is part of the Reaction and does not use your Main Action.

**Duration:** The triggering defense and immediate counterattack.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** Repeatable; normal action economy applies.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Measured Cut, Measured Guard, Interpose, Sheltering Guard, universal Shield Block.

**Combat:** Measured Cut II supplies the main attack. Sheltering Guard can use the Maneuver to protect an adjacent ally against ranged attacks. Decide whether to spend the single Reaction on Measured Guard, Interpose, Shield Block or Opportunity Attack; these are alternatives, not a stack.

**Away from combat:** Ordinary physical tasks, Ingenuity and basic teamwork remain available. This build spends most DP on battle competence rather than on a separate exploration specialty.

**Tradeoffs:** The hero wears trained light armor, not a cosmetically renamed heavy suit. Buying deeper armor training would displace attack or protection investment; heavy armor is level-3 gated. No selected feature creates a D&D-style extra turn of attacks or a renewable Second Wind heal.

**Test this:** Make both the fighter and an adjacent ally vulnerable in the same round. Can the player choose a Reaction quickly, and does each option have a reason to exist?

### Gaps kept visible

- This is a practiced sword-and-shield fighter, but has no Action Surge or Second Wind equivalent.
- Heavy armor requires HoL level 3; this build uses light armor, not a renamed heavy suit.

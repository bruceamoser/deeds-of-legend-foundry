## D&D play loop and limits

Move quickly, combine a weapon or unarmed attack with a Bonus Action strike, and spend Focus for a burst of attacks or safe repositioning.

- No subclass at level 2 under the 2024 class progression.
- No Deflect Attacks until level 3. Savage Attacker concerns weapon damage; do not automatically apply it to unarmed strikes.
- Use normal free-hand, focus, armor and component rules; stow a held weapon when casting needs that hand.

**Construction check:** standard array Str: 12; Dex: 15; Con: 13; Int: 10; Wis: 14; Cha: 8; background increases Dex: 2; Con: 1. HP calculation: 8 + 5 + 2 × CON 2. Proficiency bonus +2; initiative +5.

### Signature: Measured Guard

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, before a defense against an adjacent enemy.

Gain 1 Boon to the defense. After the attack resolves, if the defense was Strong and the attacker is within your reach, make a Basic Melee attack against that attacker. This counterattack is part of the Reaction and does not use your Main Action.

**Duration:** The triggering defense and immediate counterattack.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Use limit:** Repeatable; normal action economy applies.

## At the table

**Suggested favorites:** Measured Cut, Running Vault, Catch Your Balance, Measured Guard; keep basic unarmed attacks available without calling them learned staff techniques.

**Combat:** Use the staff for Measured Cut II, moving to satisfy Running Vault II’s lead-in before the Maneuver jump. Measured Guard can counterattack after a Strong defense, but competes with Catch Your Balance for the Reaction. It is not Flurry of Blows.

**Away from combat:** The jump and balance options support obstacle crossing. Universal climbing and other feasible tasks remain possible; no purchased climbing Boon is implied.

**Tradeoffs:** Brawn drives the learned staff attack while Agility supports mobility and defense. The high-Agility concept therefore has a real split-stat cost. No unarmored-defense progression, Focus pool, extra unarmed strikes or Stunning Strike is granted. The latter is also beyond D&D level 2.

**Test this:** Ask for a weaponless fight, then a broken bridge crossing. If the second feels right and the first does not, the missing piece is an unarmed play loop rather than more movement names.

### Gaps kept visible

- Measured Cut is used with the staff; this build does not assume weapon techniques become unarmed techniques.
- No Flurry of Blows, Focus pool, or unarmored defense progression exists in this selection. The mobile staff fighter only partially captures the monk.
- Brawn drives the staff technique despite the high Agility mobility identity.
